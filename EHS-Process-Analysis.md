# EHS Inspection and Audit Workflow Analysis

## Overview
This process map illustrates the end-to-end workflow for managing EHS (Environment, Health, and Safety) observations, from initial request to final closure. 

## Process Flow
![Inspection and Audit Process](Inspection%20and%20Audit.drawio_2.png)

## Business Impact
*   **Standardization:** Ensures consistent verification and validation steps (Level 1 & 2) across all departments.
*   **Accountability:** Clearly defines roles for the Unit EHS Officer and Fire Associate, minimizing operational bottlenecks.
*   **Compliance:** Incorporates specific CAPA (Corrective and Preventive Action) tracking, essential for regulatory audits.

## Skills Applied
*   **Process Mapping:** Translating complex operational requirements into actionable visual workflows.
*   **Requirements Gathering:** Identifying critical approval gates to maintain high-quality safety standards.
