🎯 The Challenge

A multi-division enterprise operating across manufacturing, trading, rental, and repair was running on fragmented, siloed systems. Each business unit maintained
its own workflows — disconnected from Finance, HR, and Inventory — creating a compounding set of problems:
No single source of truth for cross-divisional reporting
Manual reconciliation consuming significant analyst time weekly
Compliance tracking across GCC, EU, and India standards handled ad-hoc
Zero real-time visibility for leadership across operational KPIs
The business required a unified Odoo ERP rollout across 13 functional modules, delivered within a fixed timeline, with clearly scoped user roles, regulatory alignment, and zero disruption to live operations.
🛠 Approach
As the lead BA, I owned the full requirements lifecycle — from discovery through to FRD sign-off and UAT:
Discovery & Elicitation
Facilitated structured workshops across 6 functional departments (HR, Finance, Sales, HSE, QA/QC, Production) to surface requirements, surface conflict points, and validate scope boundaries.
Functional Requirements Design
Authored FRD V1.1 covering 13 Odoo modules end-to-end:
DomainModulesCommercialSales & CRM, Purchase, Contract Management, After SalesOperationsInventory, Manufacturing, Maintenance, Rental, LogisticsPeople & ComplianceHR & Payroll, HSE, QA/QC, Performance AssessmentFinance & ProjectsAccounting, Project Management, Document Management

Process Re-engineering
Mapped AS-IS workflows across all divisions using BPMN 2.0, identified 11 cross-functional bottlenecks, and designed TO-BE flows that eliminated redundant handoffs.

Role & Access Architecture
Defined granular user roles and permission matrices for 6 teams — ensuring least-privilege access, audit readiness, and GDPR-aligned data governance.

Agile Delivery
Managed sprint backlog in JIRA across a cross-functional team of 6. Wrote acceptance criteria per module. Led sprint reviews and maintained a live traceability matrix from requirement → test case → UAT sign-off.

Non-Functional & Compliance Coverage
Requirements explicitly mapped to scalability, security, usability, and multi-jurisdiction compliance (GCC · EU · India standards).

Most ERP BAs handle a single business function. 
This engagement required simultaneously holding the context of manufacturing ops, rental contract logic,field service workflows, and multi-jurisdiction payroll — and producing documentation precise enough that a development team in a different timezone could build from it without ambiguity.
