🎯 The Challenge

A multi-division enterprise ran on fragmented, siloed systems — disconnected across Finance, HR, Inventory, and Operations. The result: no unified reporting, manual reconciliation eating analyst hours, and ad-hoc compliance tracking across GCC, EU, and India standards.
The ask: Unified Odoo ERP across 13 modules, fixed timeline, zero disruption to live operations.

🛠 Approach

Owned the full requirements lifecycle — discovery → FRD → UAT sign-off.

Elicitation: Workshops across 6 departments (HR, Finance, Sales, HSE, QA/QC, Production)
FRD Authoring: Documented 13 modules across Commercial, Operations, People & Compliance, and Finance & Projects domains
Process Re-engineering: BPMN 2.0 AS-IS → TO-BE mapping; identified and eliminated 11 cross-functional bottlenecks
Role Architecture: Granular permission matrices for 6 teams — least-privilege access, audit-ready, GDPR-aligned
Agile Delivery: JIRA backlog management, sprint planning, acceptance criteria per module, live requirement → UAT traceability matrix
